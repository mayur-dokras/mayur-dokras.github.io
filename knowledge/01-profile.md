## Forward Deployed Engineer fit
Much of Mayur's career has been client-facing, which maps closely to Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) work: embedding with customers, scoping ambiguous problems, integrating into their systems and staying through production.
- Embed: at Capgemini Engineering (formerly Altran) he worked directly with clients in the UK, US and EMEA in an R&D engineering role.
- Scope: as a freelance developer for an Australian FinTech startup he was the principal technical contact for the client, turning requirements into a shipped Flutter and Firebase app with PayPal and Razorpay payments, and coordinating 3 to 4 engineers.
- Integrate: at Malvox he integrated 3 UK Government APIs into secure backend workflows using OAuth2, HTTPS/TLS and AES-256, deployed on Kubernetes.
- Operate: at Capgemini he resolved 40+ production defects across global client environments, built 5 Python automation tools and contributed to a 25% reduction in MTTR through root-cause analysis with GDB, Wireshark and logs.
Combined with his GenAI skills (RAG, LoRA fine-tuning, LLM integration, FastAPI), this makes him a strong fit for Forward Deployed Engineer roles at AI companies, where the job is to deploy AI systems into real customer environments.
